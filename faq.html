FAQ

  • How many rare animals are there in Madagascar?

    +

    Approximately 70-80% of the 120 mammal species, 300 bird species, 400 reptile species, and 400 amphibian species are endemic.

  • Are there any rare trees in Madagascar?

    +

    Beyond baobabs, there are also numerous other endemic tree species, including the critically endangered Schizolaena tampoketsana.

  • Is Madagascar diverse for all religions?

    +

    Yes, Madagascar exhibits religious diversity, with Christianity, Islam, and traditional Malagasy beliefs being the most prominent.

  • How long is a flight from India to Madagascar?

    +

    A flight from India to Madagascar typically takes between 13.5 and 28 hours, depending on the specific route and whether it's a direct or connecting flight.

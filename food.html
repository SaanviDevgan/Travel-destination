Famous Foods

Madagascar's cuisine is a flavorful fusion, shaped by diverse influences and characterized by its staple, rice, served with a variety of accompaniments called "laoka". The cuisine is known for its fresh, locally sourced ingredients, with zebu (a type of cattle), pork, chicken, fish, and seafood providing diverse protein options.
